## Restart the PC first

If a full restart immediately fixes the stutter, that is an important clue.

It suggests the problem is related to the sleep and resume process rather than
constant hardware performance.

## Check graphics drivers

GPU drivers are one of the first things to investigate when performance becomes
abnormal after sleep.
