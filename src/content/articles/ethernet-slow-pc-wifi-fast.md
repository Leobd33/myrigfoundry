  speed first. A damaged cable, old port, driver issue or adapter setting can
  force the connection down to a slower speed.

## First thing to check

Open your Ethernet adapter status in Windows and check the connection speed.

If you expect Gigabit Ethernet but Windows shows 100 Mbps, the cable, router port or network adapter is a strong suspect.
